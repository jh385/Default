- **Float has roughly quadrupled since the June report**: 555.6M at IPO (~4.3%) → 638.9M (~4.9%) by July → the **Aug 6 lockup unlock added 911.5M shares**, taking float to **~1.55B (~11.9% of shares out)**. Further scheduled unlocks could take float toward ~40% by mid-2027.
- **Musk**: filed Schedule 13G on **2026-08-13** disclosing **48.4% economic stake** (6.42B Class B shares, worth ~$906.9B) and retains **>82% voting power** (10:1 dual class). All of Musk's shares remain locked until 2027-06-12 — none of this stake is in the current float. Musk publicly noted on X that a meaningful share of that economic value only vests on performance milestones ("extremely crazy good outcomes").
- **Alphabet**: Q2 13F confirms **551.19M Class A shares (~$94.18B)** — unchanged share count since IPO, still the single largest institutional holder (~95% of Alphabet's own 13F portfolio by value); $80B of it is under near-term sale restriction, the rest locked through Q3 2027.
- **Vanguard**: VTI holds 18.7M shares (~$3.2B); Vanguard Extended Market ETF 6.78M shares (~$1.16B); 9 Vanguard funds hold SPCX in total. BlackRock's iShares ITOT also confirmed as a holder (share count n/a — not disclosed in sources checked).
- Broad index ETFs held roughly 14% of the (pre-unlock) free float as of late July; that percentage has likely fallen mechanically now that the float has roughly tripled, even as absolute ETF share counts hold or grow. **Morningstar counts 179 ETFs** now holding SPCX.
- **ARK Invest**: net buyer, no sales identified. Began accumulating on the Aug 5 post-earnings dip and added through the Aug 7 rally — ~317K shares across 4 funds (~$37M total). Cathie Wood has cited a "$28.5T opportunity" thesis.
- **Short interest**: no single authoritative NASDAQ settlement print was pinned down this pass; third-party trackers (Ortex/S3-style estimates cited in press) show a fast build into the unlock — roughly 31–34% of float shorted in early-to-mid July, unwinding to an estimated ~11% by Aug 13 alongside the Musk 13G filing and a broader short-covering move (per Benzinga). **Treat these figures as directional, not official** — and note the float quadrupling on Aug 6 mechanically depresses any %-of-float reading even if absolute shares short are flat.
- **Options**: launched **2026-06-16** (4 days post-IPO), debuting with 1.6M contracts traded day one — behind only TSLA/NVDA for a listing debut. Implied vol has been extraordinarily rich throughout: 172–244% at debut for near-dated contracts, now sitting around ~102% (September) declining to ~79% (2028 LEAPS) at the money. This remains some of the richest IV in the market.
