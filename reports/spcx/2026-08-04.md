## 5. Lockup status — the dominant near-term catalyst
- **Aug 6, 2026 (2 days out)**: first major tranche unlocks — the 20% base tranche, ≈**911.5M shares**, worth roughly **$100–123B** depending on which price is used. This alone would nearly **double the tradable float** (from ~555.6M to ~1.47B shares, ~11.2% of TSO).
- **Conditional +10% bonus tranche did NOT trigger.** The rule required SPCX to close ≥$175.50 (30% above the $135 IPO price) on 5 of the 10 trading days in the window ending on the earnings date (7/22–8/4). The stock spent that entire window well below $175.50 (in fact making new lows), so the bonus tranche **rolls into the later time-based tranches** rather than releasing now. This is a real de-risking versus the 6/15 report, which flagged the bonus as "currently tracking to trigger" — that read has since flipped to "did not trigger."
