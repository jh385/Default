### 🚨 Options debut — material event for today

Cboe Global Markets is listing SPCX equity options **today**, June 16 — just 4 days post-IPO (extremely fast turnaround; typical is 30+ days). Key dynamics to watch:

- **Call buying pressure:** Heavy retail interest in OTM call "lottery tickets" expected → dealers delta-hedge by buying spot → upward spot pressure
- **Implied volatility:** Likely to open very elevated (no vol history, premium for uncertainty/scarcity); watch for IV compression after the first week as the market calibrates
- **Put buyers (hedgers, Chanos-camp):** Delta-hedging puts = selling spot → partial counterforce
- **Net gamma effect:** With zero prior open interest, there is no established gamma "wall" — intraday moves may be more extreme than a stock with an established options book. First-day options activity could be a strong vol catalyst in either direction.
- **Watch the at-the-money strike:** Wherever ATM pins intraday, dealers are flat delta; above it, positive gamma feedback; below it, negative gamma feedback.
