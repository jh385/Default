## 6. Options listing — market structure event (today)

**Cboe Global Markets lists SPCX equity options today, June 16** — just 2 trading days post-IPO (faster than the typical 3–5 day window). NYSE Arca Options and Nasdaq ISE are also listed.

**Why this matters today:**

1. **Dealer delta hedging:** Market makers who sell calls will buy SPCX shares to delta-hedge. With bullish sentiment dominant, call-buying pressure should create net long delta from dealers — a marginal tailwind to the tape on open.
2. **Put availability:** For the first time since the IPO, bears can express short exposure via puts rather than share borrows (which are effectively unavailable at 4.3% float). This removes a structural demand constraint and may introduce net selling pressure if put-buying is large.
3. **Gamma zones:** The first options open interest will establish strike concentrations. Watch for call-wall buildup near round numbers ($200, $225, $250). Above the call wall, dealers are long gamma → stabilizing. Below it, they are short gamma → amplifying.
4. **Covered call writing:** Large IPO-day shareholders can now monetize by writing OTM calls. This converts upside potential to yield and creates option seller supply at higher strikes.
5. **No historical options data** exists for SPCX yet. First-day IV will be discovery-priced; expect extremely high implied volatility (IV100 likely 80–120% given float size and momentum).

**Practical watch:** First-print options chain at market open (9:30 ET). Key strikes: $200 put (support proxy), $225 call (next round resistance), $250 call (FOMO target). Volume distribution (call/put ratio) will signal whether this is a hedging open or a levered continuation rally.
