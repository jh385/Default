- **Round-trip collapse**: stock ran from ~$178 (6/15) to ATH $225.64 (6/16), then gave back the entire post-IPO gain and then some, closing below the $135 offer price for the first time in mid-July and printing a new low $119.68 on 7/20.

Wedbush's Dan Ives puts merger odds at 80% within a year; Gene Munster raised his to 90%; Kalshi prediction-market pricing is far more conservative at ~52% by May 2027. Treat this as sentiment/speculation, not confirmed corporate action — but it's a live share-price driver either way (dilution risk if SpaceX's cash burn — ~$30B this year, cumulative ~$194B through 2030 per bear case — gets folded into Tesla via a stock-for-stock deal).
- **Defense/government tailwinds**: SpaceX in talks to supply the Defense Department with AI compute capacity; SpaceX (with Rocket Lab and others) named among awardees on a $17B-ceiling Space Force contract vehicle.
- **Earnings date fixed**: first public quarterly report **Tuesday, Aug 4, 2026, after close** (call 4:30pm ET) — this is the trigger for the first lockup gate (see below). This is ~1 week earlier than the skill's original ~Aug 11 placeholder.

## Lockup status — the dominant near-term catalyst
- **First gate confirmed**: Aug 4 earnings → 2 trading days later = **August 6, 2026**, when **~911.5M shares (the 20% insider/employee tranche)** become eligible to sell. This is the "great unlocking" Bloomberg and others are now writing about; commentary pegs the gross eligible value at up to **~$116B** at recent prices.
- **Conditional +10% bonus tranche is effectively dead.** It required SPCX to close ≥$175.50 (30% above the $135 IPO price) on 5 of the 10 trading days ending on the earnings release date. With the stock stuck in the $115–$130 band through late July, that condition will not be met — one tracker headlines it plainly as "Trigger Dead." That extra 10% now rolls into the later time-based tranches instead of accelerating.
- Remaining schedule unchanged from the seed report: 7% tranches on days 70/90/105/120/135 (Aug 21 / Sep 10 / Sep 25 / Oct 10 / Oct 25), a 28% tranche tied to Q3 earnings (late Oct/early Nov), the remainder cliff Dec 8, 2026, and Musk + select insiders locked until 2027-06-12.
- Net read: the market has been pricing in the Aug 6 unlock for weeks (the "fading hype + lockup fear" narrative shows up repeatedly in press explaining the decline), so a chunk of the bad news may already be in the tape — but $116B of newly-eligible stock hitting a float this thin is still a real overhang regardless of pre-positioning.
