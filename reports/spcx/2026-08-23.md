- **Cognition AI**: SpaceX reportedly approached AI coding startup Cognition about an acquisition (following the Cursor deal); Cognition did not engage. Muted direct stock reaction — market focus stayed on the AI capex ramp instead.
- **Competitive news**: Chinese startup LandSpace landed a reusable Zhuque-3 first-stage booster (first Chinese land-pad recovery) — contributed to a down week alongside unlock jitters, though Zhuque-3's ~14.2t LEO payload is still well under Falcon 9's ~22.8t.
- **Short interest spiked dramatically**: from ~5–7% of float at IPO to reports of ~34% (219.3M shares, as of 7/29, Bloomberg) up to as high as ~45% (289.1M shares) per a real-time tracker — sources diverge meaningfully (flagged), but all agree short interest built up unusually fast for a sub-3-month-old listing.
