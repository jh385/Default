Separately, **NVIDIA disclosed a ~$21B stake** in SpaceX as of end-Q2, and reporting around Aug 18 flagged newly emerged **Google/Alphabet and BlackRock** stakes alongside Nvidia's — a real institutional-anchoring signal, and the stock popped ~5% on that news. This is incremental vs. the June report, which only had Alphabet's original allocation and ARK's initial 3.29M-share buy.
- Net read: supply overhang (lockups) and demand anchoring (index flows + strategic institutional stakes) are now visibly fighting each other in the tape, rather than being theoretical as in June.
