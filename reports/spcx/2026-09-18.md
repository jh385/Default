## 8. Catalyst watch — what's actually different from the 6/15 calendar
- **Starship Flight 14 — 9/22/26, T-4 days from this report.** This is SpaceX's **first attempt to put Starship in orbit** (target ~275km, ~6 orbits, ~10hr mission), launching ~26 V3 Starlink satellites. Musk has walked back tower-catch plans for this flight (deemed too risky this attempt); no Super Heavy booster catch either — a deliberately conservative flight profile focused on reaching orbit cleanly. This is the single biggest near-term binary catalyst on the calendar: a clean orbital insertion would be a major de-risking event for the AI/Starlink capex bull case; a failure/anomaly this close to the 9/24 lockup tranche and the Oct/Nov 28% unlock could compound selling pressure. **Escalation note: this doesn't land inside today's report window, but the 9/22 date should trigger a same-week full update regardless of tomorrow's default quick-mode call.** [techcrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/09/15/spacex-will-try-to-put-starship-in-orbit-for-the-first-time-on-september-22/), [teslaoracle](https://www.teslaoracle.com/2026/09/16/spacex-to-conduct-flight-14-starship-orbital-launch-on-september-22-no-landing-catch-is-planned/)
- **Mars transfer window (Nov–Dec 2026) — narrative has deteriorated, sourcing is contradictory.** The 6/15 report treated this as a live Nov–Dec 2026 catalyst (up to 5 uncrewed Starships). Current search results are inconsistent: some outlets still describe Nov–Dec 2026 as the live window; others (SpaceX's own site, per secondary reporting) now advertise Mars cargo flights **"no earlier than 2028."** Given Starship has not yet even reached orbit as of this report (that's what 9/22 is for), a Nov 2026 Mars departure looks operationally implausible regardless of which headline is right. **Treat the 2026 Mars catalyst as effectively de-risked/pushed out — flag for removal or heavy discounting in the next scenario table**, not as a near-term supply/demand driver. This is the most consequential single delta vs. the 6/15 report's forward calendar.
- **Insider selling / Form 144**: no single new dramatic filing surfaced beyond the scheduled tranche mechanics above; the "up to 44% of shares by early September" framing in some outlets refers to cumulative *eligibility*, not a new filing event.
- **Macro overlay**: multiple outlets flagged September's normal seasonal weakness plus ~57% odds (per CME FedWatch, late Aug) of a September Fed rate hike as a compounding near-term headwind on top of the lockup calendar — a factor the 6/15 report had no visibility into.
