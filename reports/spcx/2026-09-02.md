## What moved since June 15
1. **Q2 2026 earnings (Aug 4, after close)** — first-ever public results. Revenue $7.81B (+92% YoY vs $4.1B), beat the $6.8B Street estimate; net loss $541M (much narrower than the $1.9B loss expected); adjusted EBITDA $3.5B (+191% YoY). But **capex spiked to $18.4B** (from $10.1B the prior quarter) and the stock fell ~8% in after-hours trading despite the beat — capex intensity, not the headline numbers, is what markets are punishing. Management guided to a **$100B annualized revenue run-rate by year-end 2026**.
2. **$60B Cursor (Anysphere) acquisition** — announced June 16 (4 days after IPO), closed Aug 14. All-stock deal folding the AI-coding-assistant company into SpaceX's AI segment alongside xAI (acquired Feb 2026), as part of a vertical-integration push (Space + Connectivity + AI). JPMorgan is "increasingly positive," reaffirming a $240 PT (+~75% from spot) on the Grok/Cursor enterprise thesis. Bears (Phillip Capital, PT $75) counter that the deal adds to an already deeply negative free-cash-flow profile expected to persist through 2030, and there's been press on security/integration friction post-close.
3. **August 6 lockup event — the dominant supply story.** ~911.5M shares (~$116B notional) became technically eligible, but a large sub-tranche (~455.8M shares) **stayed locked because SPCX was trading below the $135 IPO price** — the bonus-acceleration condition from the June report (trigger level was $175.50) never came close to firing. Net effect: roughly **455M shares actually added to the float**, not the full headline amount. This is a meaningfully more bearish outcome than the June base case assumed.
4. **AI data-center reliability problems** — reporting (Seeking Alpha, Digitimes, Stocktwits) that some AI data centers (Tennessee, Mississippi) ran without backup cooling/power and suffered reliability issues; SpaceX has pulled rocket/Starlink executives (Wesley Salandro, Logan McConnell) into AI-infra leadership roles. Separately, SpaceX has signaled plans for up to **$500B in data-center capex** (24/7 Wall St, Aug 16). Market reaction so far has been muted — stock roughly flat around $143 through this news.
5. **Nasdaq-100 inclusion completed** July 7, 2026 (see Index section) — resolved from "pending" in the June report.
