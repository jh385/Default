- **ARK:** bought 3.29M shares on IPO day (~$500M+); SPCX is now ARKX's **largest holding at 8.81% weight**. No confirmed September trim/add reported.
- **Short interest — data conflicts, flagged explicitly:**
  - Free-float-based estimates (financial-media framing): short interest peaked around **~31–34% of the free float** (~196–210M shares) in late June/July, generating the "short squeeze" narrative (Musk publicly warned shorts; ~$200M of P&L per $1 move at peak).

Always check which base a source is using before comparing SPCX short-interest % to a normal large-cap.
  - As of Sep 3: borrow fee down to **0.42%**, only ~5.4M shares reported available to borrow — consistent with reporting that the % of float sold short has fallen sharply since the Aug lockup (mechanically, tripling the float alone cuts the ratio even with unchanged short share count) plus actual covering after the post-earnings rebound.
- **Net read:** the "short squeeze" setup that dominated June–July has substantially deflated — both because shares actually covered after the Aug 12 rebound and because the lockup unlocks mechanically diluted the float-based ratio. Watch the next FINRA settlement print (mid-Sept) to see which effect dominated.
