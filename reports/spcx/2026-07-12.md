## 3. What moved (since 6/15)
- **$25B bond offering** (announced 6/22–23, priced/closed ~6/29): five tranches, 2031–2056 maturities, coupons 5.35%–6.65%. Demand was strong (~$90B in orders, upsized from $20B), but equity holders read fresh leverage on top of an already-stretched valuation as a red flag — SPCX fell **>13% the week of the announcement**.
- **FY2025 / Q1 2026 financials came into view via IPO-period disclosures**: combined SpaceX+xAI posted a **$4.94B net loss for FY2025** (vs. an estimated ~$791M standalone SpaceX profit in 2024), and a **$4.28B net loss in Q1 2026 alone**. xAI is the drag — cash burn estimates range **$6.4B–$14B/year** depending on source (flagging this as an unresolved range, not a confirmed single figure). Starlink remains the profit engine (~$4.4B operating income in 2025); launch + Starlink together are profitable, xAI is not.
- **Colossus 2 legal challenge**: NAACP and environmental groups sued over unpermitted gas turbines powering the Colossus 2 data center; a shutdown order followed. This threatens the **$45B, ~3-year Anthropic compute deal** ($1.25B/month through May 2029, terminable on 90 days' notice) that runs partly through Colossus facilities. Stock briefly rebounded above $150 on relief the deal wasn't cancelled, but has since drifted back to the $145 low — this overhang is still live, not resolved.
- **Nasdaq-100 inclusion completed 7/7/26** (see index section) — the last of the three scheduled passive-inclusion events is now done.
