## 6. What's driven the ~40% drawdown since the seed report
1. **Starship V3 execution risk materialized repeatedly**: first V3 flight (May) had a Super Heavy booster separation failure; a second attempt aborted at the last second on 7/16; a 7/24 flight successfully deployed new Starlink V3 satellites but the booster exploded on a faster-than-expected water landing — the second Super Heavy failure of this generation. This directly threatens the Nov–Dec 2026 Mars transfer-window attempt.
2. **Valuation reset**: the stock was priced for perfection at the $1.77T IPO valuation; any execution stumble (see above) triggers outsized moves given zero earnings history and >100% implied vol.
3. **Cursor deal dilution overhang**: the $60B all-stock acquisition announced 6/16 diluted existing holders at a moment when post-IPO selling pressure was already building.
4. **Post-IPO reassessment / lockup positioning**: investors rotating past the "hype" phase into scrutiny of unit economics (Starlink ARPU has compressed from ~$99/mo in 2023 to ~$66/mo in Q1 2026) ahead of the first real financial disclosure.
