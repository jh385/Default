- **~8/20–21:** Second tranche (day-70, 7%, ~319M shares) unlocked; stock briefly dipped back below the $135 IPO price
- **8/21:** China's LandSpace landed a Zhuque-3 first stage — first Chinese orbital-class booster recovery — a competitive headline, not a direct SPCX driver but notable for the reusability narrative
- Underlying launch cadence is unaffected: 100th orbital mission of 2026 completed 8/21; routine Starlink/Falcon 9 launches continuing through late August

- **ARK Invest** has been a consistent dip-buyer through the unlock turbulence: +181,830 sh (~$22.8M) on 8/5 (day before the first unlock), +205,031 sh (~$27.5M) on 8/21 (day after the second unlock) — buying directly into lockup-driven weakness, funded partly by trimming Palantir/Roblox
- Net read: **heavy, growing short base + rising float from staggered unlocks vs. steady but modest active dip-buying (ARK) and passive index flows (already largely absorbed — see below).** Supply is currently winning the tug-of-war; the stock's inability to hold gains above $150 despite two "beat" catalysts (earnings, first unlock) supports that.
