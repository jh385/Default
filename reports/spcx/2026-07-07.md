## Data conflict flagged: Mars timeline
The lockup/catalyst calendar we've been carrying lists a **Nov–Dec 2026 uncrewed Mars transfer window** as a catalyst. Search results this cycle surfaced a conflicting claim that Musk announced on **2026-02-09** a 5–7 year delay to Mars ambitions in favor of lunar priorities, pushing a first Starship Mars attempt into the "early-to-mid 2030s." This predates the IPO itself, yet S-1/roadshow-era sources (May–June 2026) and our own 6/15 seed report still carried the Nov–Dec 2026 Mars window as live. We could not fully reconcile this in one pass — it's possible the "5 uncrewed ships" plan and the "5–7 year delay" statement refer to different program tracks (Mars vs. a scaled-back interim step), or one source is stale/mis-dated. **Flagging for the next research cycle**: confirm directly whether SpaceX still has an active Nov–Dec 2026 Mars attempt on the manifest, since this is a listed catalyst in our own calendar and a real ambiguity here matters for the bull case.
