Absent a sharp rally into the Aug earnings window, this 10% tranche rolls into the later time-based unlocks instead of releasing early.

Current IV percentile ~24%, i.e., options premiums are cheap relative to SPCX's own short realized-vol history — a genuine tension against the "most-shorted stock" narrative worth flagging rather than resolving; possibly reflects an early post-listing IV crush after the first few volatile weeks.
