## 5. What broke the stock since Jun 15 (the key delta)
Two idiosyncratic events, on top of the index/lockup mechanics, explain the ~26% drawdown from the $225.64 ATH:
1. **2026-06-16 Cursor/Anysphere acquisition** ($60B all-stock) — initially a positive catalyst (stock hit its ATH the same window on AI-compute narrative), but now an overhang given dilution once the deal closes in Q3 2026.
2. **SpaceX's debut bond offering** — originally sized at $20B, **upsized to $25B on ~$89B of demand**, five tranches maturing 2031–2056 (coupons 5.35%–6.65%). Despite the demand being a *positive* credit signal, the equity market read fresh $25B of debt issuance (on top of the $75–86B equity raise) as a **cash-burn / capital-intensity red flag**, and the stock's worst single day was a **-16.4%** print on the news.
3. Compounding effect: short interest built aggressively through this window (8%→13%→~31% of float), and the stock made a **fresh post-IPO low around $145–150 on 2026-07-08**, the session immediately after Nasdaq-100 inclusion (classic "sell the news").

## 8. Mars / Starship catalyst — flag a material contradiction
The standing catalyst calendar carries a **Nov–Dec 2026 uncrewed Mars transfer window** (up to 5 Starship V3 vehicles). Current reporting is genuinely split:
- Several outlets still describe SpaceX targeting the Nov–Dec 2026 window with up to five uncrewed Starships (Optimus robots, Italian Space Agency payloads).
- However, other sourcing (dated Feb 2026, pre-dating this report series) has Musk **publicly delaying Mars ambitions by 5–7 years** to prioritize lunar (HLS) work — which would push the "first Mars attempt" catalyst out of 2026 entirely.
- Given the technical requirement (40–60 total launches, incl. 8–12 tanker flights per Mars vehicle, in a brief window — a cadence never achieved by any program), the more conservative delay narrative should carry real weight. **Treat the Nov–Dec 2026 Mars catalyst as at-risk of slipping**, and watch for an explicit SpaceX schedule confirmation or denial before betting on it as a Q4 catalyst.
