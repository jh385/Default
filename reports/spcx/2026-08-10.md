## 3. Supply / demand
- **Total shares outstanding:** ~13B (>7.5B Class A + >5.5B Class B) — consistent with the 6/15 estimate.
- **Public float:** jumped from ~4.9% to **~11.8% of shares outstanding** on 8/6 when the first lockup tranche (911.5M shares) became eligible to trade — roughly **1.53B shares** now free-floating, versus ~555.6M at IPO.
- **Musk lockup:** ~6.4B Class A-equivalent shares locked through **2027-06-12**, no early-release provision — this block is untouched by any of the interim tranches.
- **Short interest:** the standout data point this cycle. Short interest has built aggressively as the stock fell — from ~40M shares (5–7% of tradable float) at IPO, to ~196M shares (~31% of float) by late July, to **~219.3M shares (~34% of float, ~$24.6B notional)** in early August. Several outlets ([Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/stocks/articles/spacex-short-sellers-build-15-133719336.html), [Benzinga](https://www.benzinga.com/etfs/specialty-etfs/26/08/60923151/spacexs-24-6-billion-short-bet-sets-up-high-stakes-week-for-leveraged-etfs)) are now framing this as short-squeeze setup material given the float is still thin relative to the short book, though the 8/6 unlock materially eases the squeeze mechanics versus pre-unlock conditions.
- **ARK Invest:** added ~210,121 shares across ARKK/ARKQ/ARKW/ARKX on 6/22, then poured another ~$52M into SPCX on 7/13 while trimming AMD and genomics names. SPCX is ~8.81% of the ARK Space & Defense Innovation ETF (ARKX).
- **Retail flow:** turned net sellers for the first time since the IPO on Friday 8/7 (-$4.5M net), after weeks of retail buying that had been credited with defending the stock through the drawdown.
- **13F:** first full-cycle 13F disclosures (covering the June 30 quarter-end) should start appearing mid-August; not yet broadly reported beyond ARK's mid-quarter buys above.

## 7. Other structural catalyst since 6/15
- **8/9–8/10: Terafab** — SpaceX and Tesla confirmed a $16.8B chip-manufacturing megaproject ("Terafab") in Grimes County, TX (Houston area), >100M sq ft, self-powered via on-site natural gas rather than ERCOT draw, ~3,000 jobs. Musk framed compute split as ~25% Tesla Optimus / ~75% SpaceX "AI spacecraft." Stock gained 2.5–6.1% on the news and it's carrying into Monday's premarket strength. This is a new diversification narrative (AI/compute) layered onto the core launch/Starlink business — but also another multi-billion-dollar capex commitment at a moment when capex is already the market's stated concern from Q2 earnings.
- **Mars timeline risk:** original catalyst calendar flagged a Nov–Dec 2026 uncrewed Mars transfer-window attempt. Musk himself walked this back in early August, tweeting it's "more likely" the first uncrewed Starships reach Mars "in ~3.5 years, next flight ~5.5 years with humans" — a materially later timeline than the "2026 window" narrative priced into the stock at IPO. Treat the Nov–Dec 2026 Mars catalyst as **likely to slip**, not confirmed.
- **Options market:** listed since late June. Day-1 ATM IV opened extreme (~169% at the 2-day expiry, ~78–102% further out) — largely dealer defense on a name nobody had hedged before. IV has since normalized to **~100% ATM (30-day), IV rank 29/100** — still elevated in absolute terms but well off the opening spike.
