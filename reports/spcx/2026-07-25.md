- **Public float:** still only **~3–5%** of shares outstanding — this thinness is repeatedly cited by outlets (Bloomberg, TheStreet, Motley Fool) as the reason SPCX swings so violently in both directions.
- **Market cap:** ~$1.57T as of 7/24 close (down from ~$2.99T at the 6/16 intraday peak — a **~$1.4T** round-trip in five weeks). [stocktwits](https://stocktwits.com/symbol/SPCX)
- **Ownership/dilution:** the Cursor deal paid $60B in Class A stock, representing **~3.4% dilution** at IPO valuation. [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/06/16/spacex-spcx-cursor-acquisition-ipo.html) Musk's ~42% economic / ~82.4% voting stake (10:1 dual class) and Alphabet's ~6–7.5% stake are unchanged as reported at IPO — no fresh 13D/13G data surfaced this cycle.
- **ARK Invest:** added further to SPCX on **7/13/26** (~$52M across its ETFs), on top of the 3.29M-share initial buy on 6/13. [ForeignPolicyJournal](https://www.foreignpolicyjournal.com/2026/07/13/ark-invest-pours-52m-into-spacex-nasdaq-spcx-while-trimming-amd-and-genomics-holdings/) No new 13F beyond ARK's has surfaced yet (first full-quarter 13F cycle for post-IPO holders is Q3, due mid-Nov).
- **Short interest — now a major story.** Estimates diverge by source but agree directionally:
  - S3 Partners: ~185M shares short, **~29% of float**, as of 7/16. [Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/stocks/articles/spacex-short-sellers-build-15-133719336.html)
  - Ortex: ~196M shares short, **~31% of float**, late July. [Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/stocks/articles/third-spacex-tradable-shares-now-145131198.html)
  - Shorts are sitting on an estimated **$15.5B unrealized paper profit**; borrow cost remains unusually low (~1%) for such a crowded short book, implying ample lendable supply (likely early institutional/IPO allocants). This is a **swelling short-squeeze setup** against the unlock — a sharp positive catalyst (earnings beat, Starship success) into thin float + heavy shorts could cut both ways violently.
  - At IPO, short interest was only ~5–7% of float (~40M shares) — the 5x buildup in 5 weeks is itself notable and consistent with bears positioning ahead of the August unlock.

## 3. Lockup status — the dominant story this cycle
This is the single biggest change since 6/15. The lockup mechanics are now **precisely dated**, not estimated:
- **Q2 2026 earnings: confirmed for August 4, 2026, after market close.** [Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/stocks/articles/spacex-sets-date-maiden-earnings-204821563.html) This replaces the "~Aug 11" placeholder from the 6/15 report and the skill's tranche calendar.
- **Unconditional first tranche: ~911.5M shares (~$116B notional) unlock on August 6, 2026** — the second trading day after earnings. [Bloomberg via Yahoo](https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/stocks/articles/spacex-great-unlocking-begins-116-145505971.html) This is being called one of the largest single lockup expirations in capital-markets history.
- **Conditional bonus tranche: 455.8M shares (~$62B notional)** unlocks only if SPCX closes at or above **$175.50 on 5 of the 10 trading days before the 8/4 earnings release.** With the stock at ~$114–119 and only ~7–8 trading days left in that window, this would require a **~50%+ rally in under two weeks** — realistically **dead** for this cycle. [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/07/21/spacex-spcx-earnings-lock-up-expiration.html)
- This does **not** mean $116B will be sold on 8/6 — it means that much stock becomes *eligible* to sell. But given the float is only ~3–5% of shares out (roughly $50–80B at current prices), even modest fractional selling from the newly-eligible pool would be a large supply shock relative to the existing float.
- Remaining schedule (unchanged from 6/15, now closer): 7% tranches on 8/21, 9/10, 9/25, 10/10, 10/25 (days 70/90/105/120/135); ~28% tranche after Q3 earnings (late Oct/early Nov); remainder on the **12/8/26** 180-day cliff; Musk + select investors locked until **6/12/27**.
- No specific Form 144 filings surfaced in this search cycle (SEC EDGAR full-text search didn't return individual insider notices) — worth checking directly at sec.gov/cgi-bin/browse-edgar closer to 8/6, since 144s are the earliest tell on actual insider intent to sell.
