## What happened in the 80-day gap (headline arc)
1. **Post-IPO melt-up**: stock ran from $161 (day-1 close) to an ATH of **$225.64 on 2026-06-16** (+67% from IPO in 4 sessions) — well past the seed report's bull-case center.
2. **Round-trip down**: gave back the entire gain and then some, falling **>50% from the ATH** and briefly trading **~20% below the $135 IPO price** in the days ahead of the August 6 lockup.
3. **Q2 2026 earnings (Aug 4, after close)**: revenue **$7.81B** (+92% YoY, beat ~$6.8–6.9B est.); net loss narrowed to **$541M** (from ~$1B); adjusted EBITDA **$3.54B** (+191% YoY). But **capex $18.37B** vs. ~$13B est. (management guided similar capex for the next two quarters) — stock fell as much as **8% after-hours** on the capex overhang.
4. **First lockup tranche (Aug 6)**: ~**911.5M shares (~20%)** became eligible. Stock printed the cycle low of **$104.83** intraday, then closed up ~6% as some of the feared selling didn't materialize immediately.
5. **10% conditional bonus tranche did NOT trigger.** It required SPCX ≥ $175.50 (30% above IPO) on 5 of the 10 sessions into the earnings date; the best close in that window was $125.33 (earnings day itself), 29% short of the bar. Result: **~455.8M shares (~58% of the entire year-end "tail") that were supposed to unlock in August instead roll into the Dec 8, 2026 cliff**, materially enlarging that date's overhang vs. what the seed report assumed.
6. **Short squeeze (~Aug 10–12)**: short interest had built to **~34%** of float into earnings/lockup (S3 Partners: ~95% of borrowable shares out on loan). As the feared post-lockup dump didn't fully materialize, shorts covered hard — stock rallied **~35% off the Aug 5 low**, briefly poking back above the $135 IPO price, and short interest collapsed to **~11%** (still a wide, possibly stale range across sources — flag).
7. **Aug 12 and Aug 20/21**: additional staggered tranches (319M shares Aug 12; the scheduled 7%/day-70 tranche around Aug 20–21) added supply on top of the above.
8. **Index inclusions completed**: MSCI USA (6/25) and Russell 1000/Top 200 (effective after close 6/26, live in the index 6/29) both went through as scheduled. **Nasdaq-100 inclusion effective 2026-07-07** at roughly a **1.3% index weight**.
9. **Starbase Louisiana**: SpaceX/Musk announced a new **$100B Starship launch and manufacturing complex** in Louisiana on 2026-08-25 (with Gov. Jeff Landry).
10. Options are now listed and trading (confirmed active options flow reported as of ~Aug 27).
