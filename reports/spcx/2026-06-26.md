### $25 Billion Bond Offering (Settling TODAY, June 26)

- **Structure:** $25B in senior unsecured notes across 5 tranches: $7.0B at 5.350% due 2031 / $6.0B at 5.650% due 2033 / $6.0B at 5.875% due 2036 / $2.5B at 6.600% due 2046 / $3.5B at 6.650% due 2056
- **Demand:** $89B in orders (3.5x oversubscribed; upsized from initial $20B)
- **Use of proceeds:** Full repayment of $20B bridge loan (4.58% effective rate); remainder for general corporate purposes
- **SpaceX cash position:** $100.8B in cash and equivalents as of June 19, 2026
- **Market reaction:** Stock initially slipped on announcement (debt concern), then recovered; premarket today +2.7%
- **Interpretation:** Strong institutional demand at investment-grade-adjacent terms signals credit credibility. Bridge loan retirement reduces refinancing risk. Net addition: ~$5B incremental long-term debt vs. retired bridge.

### Starship Flight 12 (May 22) and Flight 13 Context

- **Flight 12** (May 22, 2026, Ship 39): Partial success — suffered an ascent engine-out, was unable to perform the planned in-space engine relight, and Booster 19 had a boostback failure. Ship 39 did complete reentry and splashdown, but the mission fell short of full objectives.
- **Flight 13** (Booster 20 / Ship 40, both new V3 vehicles): NET July 31, 2026. Mission downgraded to suborbital profile given Flight 12 issues. Booster 20 cryogenic proof test completed June 7; static fire expected late June. A Flight 13 anomaly would be a meaningful negative catalyst; a clean flight would be a significant bull catalyst.
