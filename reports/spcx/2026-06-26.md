### Russell 1000 — detail

FTSE Russell's new **IPO Fast Entry rule** (adopted 2026-05-26) allows eligible large IPOs above the Russell Top 500 breakpoint to join after the 5th trading day. SPCX qualifies at pre-IPO private valuation of ~$1.75T. Classification: **90.4% growth / 9.6% value.**

This is also the inaugural **semi-annual** reconstitution — FTSE Russell moved from annual to June-and-December cadence in 2026. The $150B total Russell index reshuffle makes this a historically outsized event.

**Mechanics:** Index changes become live for trading on **Monday, June 29**. Today's close is the rebalancing moment; passive managers (IWM, VTWO, etc.) must buy SPCX at or near 4pm ET.

**Critical pattern:** The stock has fallen ~30% from its ATH since Jun 16, yet passive flows of $22–27B for Russell alone are still queued for today. The bounce from $147 low (+$10 to ~$157 premarket) may partly reflect this anticipated buying. **Watch the last 30 minutes of regular trading today extremely closely.**
