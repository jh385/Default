## 3. What moved (the 35-day delta in one paragraph)
SPCX melted up to $225.64 by 6/16 on IPO scarcity + imminent index inclusion, then gave it all back. The reversal had multiple simultaneous triggers: (1) the $25B bond sale on 6/22 revealed a chunk of the $85.7B IPO proceeds was already earmarked for debt service rather than growth capital, sending the stock down ~16% in a day; (2) the $60B all-stock Anysphere/Cursor deal raised dilution and capital-allocation concerns; (3) SpaceX's first 10-Q-equivalent disclosures showed Q1 2026 revenue of $4.7B and Starlink past 10M subscribers, but also that xAI's operating losses (~$6.4B) and heavy AI capex are offsetting Starlink's operating profit, implying a net loss; (4) options began trading 6/16 and shorts got their first on-ramp; (5) thin float (~4–5%) amplified both the melt-up and the reversal; (6) geopolitical noise (Iranian state media naming Musk's Middle East operations, including Starlink, as a potential target). None of this is a single "smoking gun" — it's a valuation reset on a richly priced, illiquid, newly-public stock hitting several catalysts at once.
