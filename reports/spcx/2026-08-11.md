- **Short interest has exploded** since IPO: ~5–7% of tradable float at listing (June) → ~31% late July (Ortex) → **~34% by early August** (S3 Partners), with ~95% of the borrow still available (i.e., not yet a hard-to-borrow squeeze setup, but roughly a third of the entire tradable float is short — an unusually large bet against the stock for a mega-cap). Shorts reportedly built a ~$15.5B mark-to-market paper profit at the July/early-Aug lows; some of that has since been given back in the post-unlock rally.
- **ARK Invest** has kept adding: ~$52M added in mid-July across ARKK/ARKQ/ARKW/ARKX, another tranche reported this week (~$36.9M), for a cumulative position of roughly 181,830 shares across the four funds. ARK's stated thesis leans on the $28.5T TAM figure from the S-1 (AI compute + Starlink), and it characterized the post-earnings selloff as an overreaction.
- No new 13F data beyond ARK's disclosed adds; first full-cycle 13F season for SPCX (institutional ownership snapshot) isn't due until mid-to-late Q3.
- **Q2 2026 earnings (Aug 4, first as a public company):** revenue $7.8B (+92% YoY, beat the $6.81B estimate); net loss narrowed to $541M (-$0.09/sh) vs. ~-$0.23–0.24 expected; adjusted EBITDA $3.5B (+191% YoY). Starlink/Connectivity segment led with $4.3B revenue and 1.7M net subscriber adds; AI segment revenue +247% YoY to $2.6B. Guidance: management points to a $100B annualized revenue run rate by end-2026 and pulled its internal $1T revenue milestone forward to 2030 (from 2031). The number that spooked the tape was **$18.4B of quarterly capex** — shares rallied ~9.4% intraday to $125.33 on the beat, then reversed hard after-hours (several sources cite an ~8.6% give-back to ~$114.60) as investors focused on spend versus the loss.
- Other flow-relevant news since the seed report: a **$1.6B Pentagon contract** (larger than all of the Space division's H1 2026 revenue) disclosed this week; Morgan Stanley's Adam Jonas called SPCX a "potential generational compounder" with a call for the stock to nearly triple by mid-2027.

## 3. Lockup status — the dominant supply story right now
This is the single biggest correction to the seed report: the first tranche unlocked on **Aug 6**, not "~Aug 11" as originally flagged as tentative.

- **Aug 6, 2026 — 20% tranche, ~911.5M shares (~$123B notional)** became eligible to sell, two trading days after the Aug 4 earnings print, per the staggered lockup structure. This is roughly **1.4x** the pre-unlock tradable float (~$86B) — an enormous supply event by any large-cap standard.
- **10% conditional bonus tranche did NOT trigger.** The bonus required SPCX to close ≥$175.50 (30% above the $135 IPO price) on 5 of the 10 trading days heading into the Aug 4 earnings date. The stock never got close (it was trading in the $110s–$130s in that window) — condition failed, so that extra supply stays locked for now.
- **Market reaction was counter-intuitive:** the stock had already priced in a lot of the selling pressure — it hit a fresh post-IPO low of $104.83 on Aug 3, three days *before* the unlock — then rallied ~6–7% on the Aug 6 unlock day itself and has continued grinding higher into the $134–139 area since. Read: heavy anticipatory de-risking into the date, some short covering afterward, and actual insider selling so far lighter/slower than the headline share count implied (notable individual sellers reported include a minor early investor, ex-NFL player Jessie Bates III, cashing out a small legacy stake).
