## 3. What moved since 6/15
- **Q2 2026 earnings (8/4):** revenue $7.8B, +92% YoY, beat the $6.72B estimate; adjusted EBITDA $3.5B (+191% YoY); net loss narrowed to $541M (EPS -$0.09 vs. -$0.26 expected). Management guided to a $100B annualized revenue run-rate by end-2026 and pulled forward its $1T revenue target to 2030 (from 2031).
- **First lockup tranche (8/6):** 911.5M shares (20% of the insider block, ~$100B notional) became eligible for sale — the single largest scheduled supply event to date. Feared selloff did not materialize: stock dipped to $104.83 intraday, then closed **+6.1%**. Free float jumped from ~4.9% to ~11.8–12% of shares out (~1.5–1.6B shares).
- **10% conditional bonus tranche did NOT trigger** — required SPCX ≥$175.50 on 5 of 10 trading days into the 8/4 earnings date; stock never closed above ~$150 in that window (was $135.27 on 7/15). That 10% rolls into the later time-based tranches instead.
- **Short interest surged into the event:** grew from ~29% of float (pre-7/17) to ~32% (per CNBC, 7/21, "Musk warns short sellers won't survive") to a reported **36% of float** by early August — SPCX's dollar short interest (~$24.6B) reportedly overtook Tesla's (~$21B), making it the more heavily shorted of the two.
- **Post-lockup short squeeze dynamics (8/6–8/7):** with the feared selloff a non-event, shorts reportedly began covering, contributing to the 8/7 rally; some desks are framing the dislocation as a long-entry opportunity (Morgan Stanley commentary at ~$1.4T market cap).
- **Index inclusions all completed** (see §5) — Russell 1000 (6/26), MSCI USA (6/25), and Nasdaq-100 (7/7, effective date confirmed) are now live; the passive-flow catalyst is behind us, not ahead.
