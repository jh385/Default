### Cursor deal dilution
SpaceX agreed June 16 to acquire Anysphere (Cursor) for **$60B in Class A stock**, expected to close Q3 2026 pending regulatory review.

- At IPO price ($135): ~3.4% dilution (444M new shares)
- At current ~$200/share: ~2.3% dilution (~300M new shares)
- These shares flow to Anysphere shareholders (founders + VC investors) with deal-specific lockup — **zero immediate float impact**
- Medium-term (2027+): eventual additional supply of ~300M shares — roughly 54% of current public float

Morningstar characterizes the deal as **value-neutral** (lowered FV estimate from $63 → $62). Pitchbook notes SpaceX used IPO stock up 50%+ from offer price as "super-currency," avoiding cash or debt to fund the purchase.

## 5. Cursor / Anysphere Deal — Deep Dive

**Deal announced**: June 16, 2026 (same session as ATH $225.64)
**Price**: $60B all-stock
**Target**: Anysphere — maker of **Cursor**, an AI coding assistant
**Growth**: ARR $100M (early 2025) → **$4B+ (June 2026)**; one of the fastest-ever SaaS ramp-ups
**Close**: Targeted Q3 2026, pending regulatory review (FTC/DOJ scrutiny likely)
**Largest-ever**: Biggest VC-backed startup acquisition on record

**Bull case on deal**: 
- Cursor integrates with SpaceX Colossus data centers (GPU infrastructure) 
- Completes xAI ecosystem (SpaceX + xAI + Cursor = vertically integrated AI stack)
- $4B ARR growing fast — Morningstar sees this as value-neutral at worst; bull case is strategic home run
- Elon used post-IPO premium stock rather than cash or debt — extremely capital-efficient

**Bear case on deal**:
- 15× revenue multiple ($60B / $4B ARR) is historically stretched even for high-growth AI
- SpaceX's core competencies are launch services and satellite comms — AI coding tool is a departure
- All-stock deal means Cursor shareholders are motivated SpaceX sellers post-lockup
- Regulatory risk: SpaceX AI + xAI + Cursor could attract antitrust scrutiny
- Morningstar FV estimate is $63 vs. current $209+ — deal merely keeps pace with an already-expensive valuation

**Market reaction**:
- June 16 (deal day): SPCX +~16% intraday → ATH $225.64 (closed lower ~$201)
- June 17 (first down day): −4.95% → $191.82 — "locked-up shares and missing shorts skewed debut" (StockTwits)
- June 18 premarket: +9.4% → $209.86 — recovery/reassessment
