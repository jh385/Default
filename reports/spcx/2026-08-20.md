## 3. What moved since June 15
The stock has been on a round trip: it rallied from ~$178 (June 15) to an ATH of $225.64 on June 16, then round-tripped all the way down to a post-IPO low of $104.83 by August 3 as the market priced in the coming lockup wave and macro pressure on high-beta tech/growth names. First-ever earnings (Aug 4) triggered a violent whipsaw — initial +9.4% on a revenue beat, then an ‑8.6% after-hours reversal on capex sticker shock, followed by a ~23% two-day rally (Aug 6–7) worth >$327B in market cap as the first lockup tranche cleared without a flood of selling. Shares reclaimed the $135 IPO price on Aug 10 for the first time since mid-July, but have slid back to the low $130s this week as the **second lockup tranche (~319M shares, ~7% of the insider block) becomes eligible for sale today, Aug 20**, compounding a broader growth-stock selloff.
