  ~7x the original ~555.6M IPO float) but a precise post-unlock float % was not

- **Short interest: ~34% peak (into the Aug 4 earnings / Aug 6 unlock) → ~11%
  by mid-August** as shorts covered into the float expansion and rally.
  [(CNBC)](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/08/12/spacex-short-sellers-are-running-out-of-bullets-as-stock-rebounds-38percent-off-low.html)
- **Rule 144 sale cap**: affiliate sales are capped each 3-month period at the
  greater of 1% of Class A shares outstanding or 4-week average weekly volume;
  at SPCX's turnover the volume test governs and permits roughly ~378M shares/
  quarter of affiliate selling — a real throttle on how fast unlocked shares can
  actually hit the tape even as nominal tranches get larger.
- **13F (positions as of 6/30/26, filed 8/14/26): 474 institutional 13F/13D-G
  filers, ~36.6M shares combined** (this is a small slice of the ~13B share
  count and predates the Aug 6 float expansion — next 13F cycle, due mid-Nov
  for 9/30/26 positions, will be the first to show post-unlock institutional
  positioning).
- **ARK Invest**: continues to add — ~$52M in July, ~$37M more in August during
  post-earnings volatility; SPCX is a top holding in ARKX and one of ARKK's
  largest positions.
- Musk economic stake (~42%) / voting control (~82.4% via 10:1 dual class)
  unchanged as reported at IPO; no Musk-specific Form 144 activity surfaced in
  this pass (Musk's own shares are locked until the 366-day cliff, 2027-06-12).
