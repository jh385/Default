## Starship / Mars program
- Flight 12 and Flight 13 (both Version 3) flew in July; Flight 13 (7/24) was the first V3 flight to survive reentry and splash down intact since late 2024, and deployed the most advanced Starlink satellites flown to date
- Flight 14 is targeted for **late August 2026** and is meant to deploy viable orbital payloads for the first time — a genuine technical milestone, not just a test hop
- On the Q2 earnings call, Musk reiterated wanting to launch (and catch) the next Starship "this month"
- **Mars transfer window (Nov–Dec 2026)**: still the target for an uncrewed Starship Mars attempt, contingent on continued clean test flights through the fall — this remains the single largest binary catalyst left on the 2026 calendar
