## 2. Supply / demand
- TSO (Class A + B): **~13.08B** shares (unchanged); public float still **~555.6M (~4–5%)** — among the thinnest large-cap floats ever.

This is a **credit-market red flag** worth watching as a leading indicator of institutional sentiment on the equity.
- **Short interest has exploded**: from ~13% of free float in mid-June to **~31% of free float (~196M shares)** as of ~July 1, per Ortex — more than doubling in days. Borrow cost has simultaneously **collapsed from ~14% at launch to ~1%**, meaning shares are newly easy to locate even as the short base has ballooned. That combination (huge short base + cheap borrow + ~4–5% float) is a textbook **squeeze setup**, not a bearish "smart money is right" signal on its own — flagged as the single most important supply/demand fact this cycle.
- ARK / 13F: no fresh 13F cycle has posted since the seed report (next 13F season is mid-August for Q2); ARK's initial 3.29M-share buy (6/13) is the only confirmed data point still.
- Musk: ~42% economic / 82.4% voting (unchanged, 10:1 dual class). Alphabet ~6–7.5% (unchanged).
- FAA added SpaceX (along with Blue Origin, Rocket Lab, Virgin Galactic) to its **prohibited-investment list** for FAA employees/spouses/minor children, given the agency's licensing/oversight role over SpaceX launches. This removes a small pool of potential buyers but is more a governance/optics item than a flow-moving one.
