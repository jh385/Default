  - Investing.com's estimates page shows **Aug 6**.
  - Multiple lockup-tracker sites (StockAlarm, Darrow Wealth, KuCoin) still say "late July/early August," consistent with the 6/15 report's ~Aug 11 estimate.
  - **Flagging this as an unresolved conflict** — treat July 11 as unconfirmed until SpaceX IR posts a date.
- **Tranche-1 size is also now disputed**: the 6/15 report (and most trackers) describe **20% + a conditional 10% bonus**, releasing 2 trading days after Q2 earnings. The 20%+10% framing is corroborated by more sources and the original S-1 secondary reporting, so it remains the base case, but this discrepancy wasn't resolved and is worth another look once SpaceX files an 8-K confirming the earnings date.
- **The +10% bonus tranche is now tracking to MISS its trigger.** The condition is closing ≥$175.50 (30% over the $135 IPO price) on 5 of the 10 trading days before earnings. SPCX blew through $175.50 in mid-June (new ATH $225.64) but the 6/19–6/22 crash took it back down through that level, and it's traded in the $160–$171 band since. Unless it stages a sharp rally into whatever the earnings date turns out to be, that 10% likely rolls into the later time-based tranches instead of releasing early — modestly bullish for near-term supply, since it delays ~650M shares.
- Rest of the schedule (7% tranches Aug 21/Sep 10/Sep 25/Oct 10/Oct 25, 28% at Q3 earnings, remainder Dec 8, Musk/select investors June 12 2027) is unchanged from 6/15.
