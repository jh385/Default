Most likely: **announcement around June 17 (day 3); effective date June 25 or June 29.** Treat as ±4 day range; both are this week.

### MSCI ESG Rating — New Development Since June 15

SpaceX received **MSCI's lowest possible ESG rating (CCC)**, comparable to the rating assigned to Russia following the Ukraine invasion. This designation:

- **Excludes SPCX** from MSCI ESG Leaders Index, MSCI ESG Screened, and all ESG-filtered products
- Reduces total passive mechanical demand by an estimated **$3–5B** vs. prior $22–30B aggregate estimate
- ESG-mandated institutional investors (pension funds, sovereign wealth funds with ESG policy) may actively **exclude** SPCX from portfolios
- Rating linked to environmental governance of rocket launches, dual-class share structure governance discount, and (per media) Musk commentary on energy/propulsion

**Revised total passive demand estimate: ~$17–26B** (vs. $22–30B on June 15), with the ESG-excluded tranche being the primary reduction.
