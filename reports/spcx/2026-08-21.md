## Q2 earnings (reported 2026-08-04, after close)
Confirmed by 4+ independent sources ([SpaceX IR](https://ir.spacex.com/events/event-details/2026/SpaceX-Q2-2026-Earnings/default.aspx), [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/08/04/spacex-spcx-earnings-live-updates-q2-2026.html), [Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/stocks/articles/spacex-q2-2026-earnings-revenue-205130498.html), [Shacknews](https://www.shacknews.com/article/150244/spacex-spcx-q2-2026-earnings-results)) — this was the single most-consistent fact found in this research pass:
- Revenue **$7.8B, +92% YoY**, beat estimates of ~$6.7–6.8B.
- Net loss **$541M** (narrowed from ~$1B a year ago); EPS ‑$0.09 vs. ‑$0.26 expected.
- Adjusted EBITDA **$3.5B, +191% YoY**.
- Capex **$18.4B** vs. ~$13B expected — the capex overshoot is what spooked the market, not the top line.
- Segment detail: a disclosed **"AI segment" (xAI / X / cloud services)** at $2.56B revenue, +247% YoY; **Connectivity (Starlink)** +66% revenue growth on a doubling subscriber base.
- Reaction: shares fell as much as **8% after-hours** on the capex concern despite the beat.
