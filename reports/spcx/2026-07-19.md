## 4. Lockup status — the central story of this report

- **First earnings date is now confirmed for 2026-08-06** (Q2 2026, first as a public company) —
  [MarketBeat](https://www.marketbeat.com/stocks/NASDAQ/SPCX/earnings/), [Moomoo community calendar](https://www.moomoo.com/community/feed/spacex-spcx-us-next-earnings-q2-2026-estimated-window-late-116896309248006). This is firmer and about 5 days earlier than
  the 6/15 report's ~8/11 estimate.
- **First lockup release is ~2 trading days after earnings** (~8/8–8/10): **911.5M shares**
  unlock, equal to ~6.97% of total shares outstanding — [Investing.com](https://www.investing.com/news/stock-market-news/spacex-ipo-lockup-expiry-123b-in-shares-set-to-unlock-in-early-august-2026-93CH-4796311), [BiyaPay](https://www.biyapay.com/en/blogdetail/4087-spacex-spcx-lockup-expiration-schedule-20262027-fl). At the current price this tranche
  alone is worth roughly **$113B** in newly tradable stock, against a current float of only ~646M
  shares — i.e. this single unlock is larger than the entire current tradable float. (The "20%"
  figure in the 6/15 report refers to 20% of the *lockup-restricted share pool*, not of total TSO;
  911.5M / ~13.08B ≈ 7%, consistent once you note the restricted pool itself is a subset of TSO.)
- **The bonus 10% "30% rule" tranche has effectively been missed.** The provision required SPCX to
  close ≥$175.50 (30% above the $135 IPO price) on 5 of the 10 trading days ending on the earnings
  date. SPCX closed at $135.27 on 7/15 — already 23% *below* the trigger — and has since fallen
  further — [Benzinga](https://www.benzinga.com/trading-ideas/long-ideas/26/07/60286823/spacex-has-a-hidden-30-rule-and-it-could-surprise-investors), [BeInCrypto](https://beincrypto.com/spacex-stock-struggles-earnings-share-unlocks/). With five trading days or fewer left before 8/6 and the stock ~$50 under the
  bar, this bonus 456M-share early release will **not** trigger; it rolls into the later
  time-based tranches instead. This is a genuine (if modest) net positive for near-term supply
  versus the 6/15 report, which had this tracking toward triggering.
- Remaining schedule (7% tranches 8/21, 9/10, 9/25, 10/10, 10/25; ~28% at Q3 earnings late
  Oct/early Nov; remainder at the 12/8 180-day cliff; Musk + select investors 6/12/2027) is
  unchanged and not re-verified line-by-line this cycle.
- Market framing has shifted from "lockups are a future risk" (6/15) to "lockups are the reason
  for the current selloff" (7/19) — multiple outlets now explicitly link the stock's slide below
  IPO price to positioning ahead of the 8/6–8/8 unlock — [Axios](https://www.axios.com/2026/07/17/spacex-lockup-stock-selloff), [Investing.com](https://www.investing.com/news/stock-market-news/spacex-ipo-lockup-expiry-123b-in-shares-set-to-unlock-in-early-august-2026-93CH-4796311).
