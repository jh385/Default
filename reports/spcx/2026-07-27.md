## 5. Lockup status — the dominant story right now
- **Confirmed date: Aug 4 earnings (after close) → unlock window opens Aug 6** (2nd full trading day post-release), not the "~Aug 11" placeholder from the June seed report — the actual trigger is tied directly to the earnings date, now locked in.
- **Base tranche**: up to 20% of insider/restricted holdings eligible to sell, reported as up to **911.5M shares**, worth as much as **~$109–123B** at recent prices (estimates vary by source/price-date: Investing.com "$123B," CNBC coverage frames it as ~1.45x the size of the original IPO).
- **Bonus 10% tranche (455.8M add'l shares)**: conditional on SPCX closing ≥**$175.50** (30% above the $135 IPO price) on 5 of the 10 trading days into the Aug 4 report. **Not on track** — stock closed at $135.27 on 7/15 (already 23% below trigger) and has fallen further since (~$115 now, ~34% below trigger). This tranche is effectively dead for this cycle; would need a >50% rally in ~1 week to have any path, which isn't in any forecast.
- Rest of schedule unchanged from June report: 7% tranches Aug 21 / Sep 10 / Sep 25 / Oct 10 / Oct 25 (days 70/90/105/120/135); 28% at Q3 earnings (late Oct/early Nov); remainder Dec 8 (day 180); Musk + select investors June 12, 2027 (day 366).
- **Net read**: the market is already pricing in this unlock — the stock's slide from $225 to sub-$115 over six weeks is in substantial part a pre-positioning move ahead of Aug 6. Whether the actual unlock is a "sell the rumor, buy the news" event or a second leg down depends on how much of the 911.5M-share eligible pool insiders actually list (most lockup unlocks see far less than 100% sold on day one) — no data yet on insider intent (no Form 144s found in an EDGAR text search this run; they typically file within days of the eligibility date, so watch for a batch starting ~Aug 4–6).
