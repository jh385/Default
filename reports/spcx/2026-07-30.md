## Identification check

## Supply / demand
- Shares outstanding: **~13.08–13.2B** (Class A + Class B combined; minor variance across sources,
  unchanged from IPO structure).
- Public float: still **~4–5%** of shares out (~550–650M shares) — the "puny free float" that
  index inclusion and short sellers are both fighting over. No change until the Aug 6 unlock.
- **Short interest has exploded**: from an estimated 5–7% of float three weeks ago to **~29–32% of
  float** now (~185–206M shares short, ~$25B notional, per S3 Partners / Ortex). This is one of the
  largest short bases relative to float of any mega-cap listing on record — a function of the tiny
  float meeting heavy bearish conviction on lockup/valuation grounds. Musk has publicly warned short
  sellers "won't survive," setting up asymmetric squeeze risk if the Aug 6 unlock disappoints to the
  upside (i.e., sells less than feared).
- ARK Invest continues to buy the dip: ~$52M added mid-July, another ~$21.3M on 7/27 (incl. a
  single-day $15M / 121,384-share buy across ARKK, ARKQ, ARKW, ARKX). SPCX is now ~4.5% of ARK's
  aggregate invested assets — conviction-sized, not a token position.
- Congressional stock-purchase controversy: six House members (or families) bought $83K–$245K of
  SPCX within 6 days of the IPO; 5 of 6 sit on committees overseeing SpaceX-relevant contracts. House
  passed the Stop Insider Trading Act (7/22) partly in response. Not a direct price driver, but adds
  headline/regulatory-attention risk.
- Insider Form 4/144 activity: no fresh insider sales of consequence found (insiders remain locked up
  until Aug 6) — one stale ~$1.2M sale over the trailing 12 months per MarketBeat, pre-dating the
  active lockup period. The real test starts next week.

## Lockup status (the dominant near-term catalyst)
- **Q2 2026 earnings confirmed: Tuesday, Aug 4, after close** (webcast 4:30pm ET) — the maiden
  public earnings report.
- **First lockup tranche: Aug 6** (2nd full trading day after earnings) — **~911.5M shares** (~20% of
  the insider block, ~7% of total shares out) become eligible to sell. At the current price (~$112.55)
  that's **~$103B** of newly tradable stock; headlines from earlier in July cited $116–123B when the
  stock was higher — the dollar figure is moving down with the price, not a discrepancy.
- **The +10% conditional bonus tranche has NOT triggered.** It required SPCX to close >30% above the
  $135 IPO price (i.e., ≥$175.50) on 5 of the 10 sessions before earnings. The stock has traded well
  below that band all month (intraday low $107.01 on 7/28), so only the base 20% unlocks — a reversal
  from the 6/15 report, when the stock was tracking to trigger the bonus.
- Confirmed unchanged: 7% tranches on day 70/90/105/120/135 (Aug 21, Sep 10, Sep 25, Oct 10, Oct 25);
  28% post-Q3-earnings release (late Oct/early Nov, the single largest unlock); remainder at the
  Dec 8 180-day cliff; Musk + select investors at the Jun 12, 2027 366-day cliff. Musk's ~6.4B shares
  are excluded from every early-release provision — he remains locked out to 2027.
- KeyBanc's independent estimate (~11% of shares out eligible around Q2 earnings) is roughly
  consistent with the ~7%-of-total / ~20%-of-insider-block framing above once you account for the
  denominator difference.
