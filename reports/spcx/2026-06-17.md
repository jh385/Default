### Options — Day 2 of trading

Options listed June 16 on the CBOE. Debut described as "historic" with structural anomalies:

- **IV level:** Pricing ±25% monthly move. No historical IV to anchor against — options market
  is flying blind on term structure.
- **Retail flow:** Heavy retail call buying from the first session; dealers almost certainly
  short gamma on the net across the board.
- **Gamma squeeze dynamics:** With a 3–5% tradeable float, dealer hedging of net short gamma
  positions has a direct and amplified transmission into SPCX spot price. A rally forces
  dealers to buy shares to hedge calls; buying feeds more rally; repeat.
- **Extreme bull case ($400):** Floated by ZeroHedge, Benzinga, and crypto-adjacent media
  — driven by the float squeeze logic. Not a consensus view; requires sustained retail
  call accumulation and no large-block supply to absorb dealer buying.
- **Key risk for options buyers:** IV crush. Premiums are historically extreme with no
  anchor; any volatility mean-reversion will shred long options value even on correct
  directional calls. Wide bid-ask spreads make round-trips costly.
- **Put/call ratio:** IV Rank reported at 24% (tradestie) — counterintuitively low, likely
  because there is no historical range to compare against; treat as unreliable.

## Cursor / Anysphere acquisition — material deep-dive

Announced June 16 via 8-K. Key terms:
- **Structure:** Agreement and Plan of Merger; Cursor becomes wholly owned SpaceX subsidiary
  via X67 Inc. (SpaceX sub)
- **Valuation:** $60B implied equity value
- **Consideration:** 100% all-stock — each share of Cursor common/preferred converts into
  SPCX Class A at exchange ratio based on **7-day VWAP of SPCX before deal close**
- **Expected close:** Q3 2026
- **Cursor metrics at signing:** >$4B annualized revenue (~$2.6B from B2B enterprise);
  massive growth rate
- **Strategic fit:** Third AI/tech acquisition (after xAI merger). SpaceX is evolving from
  a pure-play aerospace company into a tech conglomerate (Starlink + xAI + Cursor)

**Bull case:** Cursor's $4B ARR is real revenue that starts flowing into SpaceX financials
post-close. The xAI + Cursor stack gives SpaceX end-to-end AI infrastructure. Bill Ackman:
"The Cursor acquisition costs materially less in dilution because of SpaceX's high valuation."

**Bear case:** Morningstar lowered fair value to $62/share (from $63) — calling the stock
"more expensive" after the deal, not less. The $60B price for $4B ARR implies a ~15× revenue
multiple with no clear synergy roadmap. Dilution hits existing holders, and Cursor investors
add a new wave of SPCX holders with incentive to sell. If integration underperforms, holders
absorb the dilution with no financial payoff.

**Dilution timeline:** ~3–4% dilution lands when the deal closes in Q3 2026. Combined with
lockup tranches starting Aug 11, Q3 could become the first significant supply pressure period.
