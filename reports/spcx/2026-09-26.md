## Near-term catalysts
- **Starship Flight 14 — as soon as Monday 2026-09-28** (pending FAA approval; originally 9/22). First orbital flight of the V3 stack; will deliver 26 production Starlink V3 satellites — SpaceX's CFO has called this the vehicle's first *revenue-generating* mission. Booster and ship are expendable/splashdown on this flight (no catch attempt). This is the single biggest binary event in the next week and sits just 2 days after this report.
- **NASA:** awarded SpaceX another $950M (9/18) for 3 more crewed ISS flights — incremental positive on the core launch/crew business.
- **AI/compute:** Grok Bot agent surpassed 400K weekly users (9/23); Mizuho reiterated SpaceX can sustain premium compute pricing through 2027 — supports the AI-segment bull case that drove the +247% YoY AI revenue growth in Q2.
- **Mars window (Nov–Dec 2026):** conflicting signals. Base case remains up to 5 uncrewed Starship Mars attempts in the Nov–Dec transfer window (next window not until 2029). But at least one recent report has Musk calling the 2026 Mars attempt "a distraction" that SpaceX won't pursue, and Musk has separately put the odds of meeting the orbital-refueling prerequisite at "50/50." **Flag: this materially softens the Mars catalyst versus the June report's framing** — treat the Mars mission as at-risk/uncertain rather than a scheduled event.
- **Q3 earnings (~Nov 3–5, unconfirmed):** triggers the 28%/~1.3B-share unlock — the largest single supply event on the calendar and the next real test of demand absorption.
