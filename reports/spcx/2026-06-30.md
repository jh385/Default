## 3. What moved since Jun 15 (15-day recap)

### Jun 16 — Cursor acquisition ($60B all-stock) + ATH $225.64
SpaceX announced a [$60B all-stock deal to acquire AI coding startup Cursor](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/06/16/spacex-spcx-cursor-acquisition-ipo.html) — just 4 days after IPO. Cursor had $1B+ in ARR as of late 2025. The deal represents **3.4% dilution** to fully diluted share count (~445M new Class A shares at IPO valuation). Paradoxically, the stock also hit its ATH of **$225.64** on this day before the dilution math was digested. Former Nasdaq chief warned the stock was "not trading on fundamentals."

### Jun 22 — $20B bond offering
SpaceX issued $20B in corporate bonds. The [offering drew $89B in demand (4.45x oversubscribed)](https://www.tradingkey.com/analysis/stocks/us-stocks/261986935-spcx-stock-analysis-bond-demand-june-2026-tradingkey) — strong credit market confidence. Equity market interpreted the raise as a sign of ongoing cash consumption; stock sold off further.

### Jun 23 — -16% single-session washout, 52-week low $147.11
A [combination of the Cursor dilution, bond offering uncertainty, broader macro pressure, and Fed rate expectations](https://fortune.com/2026/06/23/stock-markets-fed-interest-rate-expectations-400-billion-spacex/) drove SPCX to $147.11 — wiping ~$400B in market cap. Short sellers entered (briefly 13% of float intraday; normalized 5–7%). [ARK Invest bought the dip: +210,121 shares (~$32.5M)](https://www.kucoin.com/news/flash/ark-invest-buys-32-5m-in-spacex-shares-after-16-spcx-drop) across ARKK, ARKQ, ARKW, ARKX.

### Jun 25/26 — MSCI USA + Russell 1000 inclusions effective
- [MSCI USA inclusion effective](https://andrew.ooo/answers/spcx-msci-index-inclusion-what-it-means-june-29-2026/) ~Jun 25–29 (date conflicts between sources; confirmed done as of today)
- [Russell 1000 / Russell Top 200 effective after close Jun 26](https://www.gurufocus.com/news/8934029/ftse-russell-annual-index-rebalance-spacex-coreweave-join-russell-1000)
- Passive buying provided a floor; stock bounced from $147 lows into $153–$166 range

### Jun 26 — Charter Communications mobile partnership leak
[Bloomberg reported executive-level talks between SpaceX and Charter Communications](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-06-26/spacex-charter-discussed-mobile-phone-partnership-in-us) on a consumer mobile phone offering. Charter would run Starlink traffic through its ground network (similar to existing Spectrum Mobile model), positioning SpaceX to compete with Verizon, AT&T, and T-Mobile. Neither party confirmed. [CHTR surged +24% on June 29](https://invezz.com/ng/news/2026/06/29/charter-communications-stock-jumps-over-24percent-whats-the-spacex-link/).

### Jun 30 (today) — +7.2% breakout
Two concurrent catalysts:
1. **Nasdaq-100 inclusion confirmed for July 7**: [J.P. Morgan estimates ~$4.3B passive inflow](https://www.ebc.com/forex/spacex-stock-nasdaq-100-index-trade). Fastest Nasdaq-100 inclusion in history (15 trading days post-listing). Pre-positioning ahead of July 7 effective date is driving buying.
2. **Charter deal newsflow continuing**: Speculation of imminent deal terms announcement.

Stock has broken back above its Jun 12 day-1 close anchor ($160.95) for the first time since the Jun 22–23 washout.
