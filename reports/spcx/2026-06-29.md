### Bonus Trigger — Now At Risk

The +10% bonus tranche unlocks only if SPCX closes **≥ $175.50 on at least 5 of the 10 trading days** ending on the August 11 Q2 earnings date. The measurement window runs approximately **July 28 – August 8**.

**Current premarket: $150.65 — 16.5% below the $175.50 trigger.**

The stock hit $225.64 on June 16 (well above the threshold), but fully retraced after the bond sale announcement. At $150.65, SPCX needs a **+16.5% recovery within the next ~29 trading days** and must sustain it through 5 of the 10 days before earnings. Estimated probability the bonus triggers from current levels: **~25–30%** — possible on a Starship catalyst or NDX flow surge, but not the base case.

**Strategic significance:** If the bonus triggers, insiders who benefit from the 10% early bonus have greater incentive to *support* the stock into earnings rather than front-run selling. If it misses, the Aug 13 unlock is a straight 20% flood with no insider alignment mechanism operating.

**Lockup supply magnitude:** 20% of the ~12.5B locked insider block = **~2.5B shares** on Aug 13. Current float is ~625M. This is a **4× float expansion in one day** — the dominant supply event of 2026. Even at $135/share (IPO floor), 2.5B shares = ~$337B in potential sell-side supply. Demand must come from index mandates, retail, and new institutional entrants to absorb this.

**Form 144 filings:** None yet. First expected around late July (insiders legally permitted to plan sales ahead of unlock window). Monitor SEC EDGAR daily from late July.
